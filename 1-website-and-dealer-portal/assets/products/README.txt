PRODUCT PICTURES (single source)

Every product picture shown in the dealer portal and the staff console is loaded from THIS folder on the website:
  assets/products/<product id>.webp

To use the brand-website picture of a product, save it here with exactly that file name (webp, about 600 px wide) and replace the old file. Nothing else needs changing.
The portal no longer contains any product pictures of its own.
